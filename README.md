# CollabNext

### *The Collaborative AI Editor for Filmmakers & Content Creators*

[Demo Link](https://youtu.be/V7JPosz7kVw)


**CollabNext** is a next-generation **AI-powered collaborative editor** built specifically for filmmakers, writers, and content creators.
It redefines how AI assists in storytelling making it **a seamless, creative partner** rather than an intrusive tool.


## Overview

CollabNext is designed around the principle:

> **AI should feel like a natural part of the creative process helpful, intuitive, and unobtrusive.**

---

##  Key Features

### AI Writing Assistance

* **Summarization:** Quickly generate summaries of long scenes, episodes, or scripts.
* **Translation:** Instantly translate your script into multiple languages for global collaboration.
* **Tone Adjustment:** Adapt your writing style cinematic, emotional, formal, or casual in one click.
* **Ghost Writer:** Predicts and suggests the next few words or sentences based on full-document context, helping you stay in the flow.

---

### Story & Comic Tools

* **AI Script Generation:** Automatically create scene-by-scene or panel-by-panel scripts for comics or storyboards.
* **Editable AI Scripts:** Review, edit, and refine AI-generated content to match your style before generating visuals.
* **Story Analyzer:** Get detailed plot summaries, character insights, and predicted story progressions a dream tool for scriptwriters and editors.

---

### Collaboration & Creativity

* **Collaborative Whiteboard:** Sketch and brainstorm ideas with your team in real time.
* **Sketch-to-Image Generation:** Turn rough whiteboard sketches into polished visuals using generative AI.
* **Real-Time Co-Creation:** Sync scripts, visuals, and notes seamlessly across multiple users and devices.
